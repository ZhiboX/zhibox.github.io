Practitioner

Dr Sherman Gu

Dr Sherman Gu graduated from Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine in 1982 and later completed postgraduate study at RMIT University in Melbourne. He has more than 35 years of clinical experience.

  • Bachelor’s Degree of Medicine, Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine
  • Advanced Diploma of Myotherapy, RMIT University
  • Master Degree of Applied Science, RMIT University
  • PhD, RMIT University
  • Registered in acupuncture and Chinese herbal medicine
  • AHPRA registration: CMR0001738861
  • Member, Federation of Chinese Medicine & Acupuncture Societies of Australia (FCMA)
