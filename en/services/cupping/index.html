Cupping Therapy

Cupping Therapy in Melbourne

Cupping is a traditional technique that uses suction cups on the skin. At Knox Chinese Medicine it may be used as part of a treatment plan for muscular tension, mobility or relaxation-related goals.

Types of cupping described by the clinic

Fixed cupping

Cups are positioned on selected areas and remain in place for a period of time.

Moving cupping

Oil or cream may be applied so cups can be moved over selected skin areas.

Dynamic cupping

Silicone cups may be combined with movement or muscle activation depending on the treatment goal.

Why patients may enquire about cupping

  • Muscle tension or stiffness
  • Relaxation
  • Mobility and flexibility goals
  • Sports and exercise recovery
Cupping commonly leaves temporary circular marks or bruising. Tell the practitioner about blood-thinning medicines, bleeding disorders, skin problems, pregnancy or other relevant medical conditions before treatment.

Cupping FAQs

Does cupping hurt?

People commonly feel pulling or pressure from the suction. Temporary marks can occur. Tell the practitioner if anything feels uncomfortable.

How long does a session last?

A typical cupping session may be around 20–30 minutes, depending on the treatment plan.

Is cupping safe?

Risk depends on the person and technique. Cupping should be performed using appropriate hygiene and by a practitioner who can assess contraindications and precautions.

How many sessions are needed?

There is no single number for everyone. The practitioner can discuss frequency based on symptoms, response and treatment goals.
