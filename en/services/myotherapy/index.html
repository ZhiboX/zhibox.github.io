Myotherapy

Myotherapy

Myotherapy is a soft-tissue therapy used in the assessment and management of muscular and movement-related concerns. Techniques may include soft-tissue massage, stretching, corrective exercise, electrical stimulation, dry needling and patient education.

About myotherapy at the clinic

Treatment is selected according to assessment and may involve one or more soft-tissue and movement-management techniques. The clinic provides a published research reference in relation to myofascial treatment.

View the research reference on PubMed →

Myotherapy should not replace medical assessment where symptoms may indicate an injury or medical condition requiring diagnosis or urgent care.
