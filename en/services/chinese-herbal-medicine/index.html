How the service works

Comprehensive consultation

An assessment is used to understand symptoms, history and treatment goals.

Individualised formulas

Herbal formulas are selected for the individual rather than supplied as a one-size-fits-all treatment.

Herbal formats

Available formats may include granulated powders, pills and capsules.

Combined care

Where appropriate, herbal medicine may be discussed alongside acupuncture or cupping.

Common reasons patients enquire

People may enquire about herbal medicine for colds and flu symptoms, menstrual symptoms, menopause, digestive problems, sleep problems, stress and anxiety, and chronic pain or inflammation. Whether herbal medicine is appropriate depends on the individual assessment.

Types of herbal preparations

Granulated herbs

Granulated preparations are concentrated herbal powders designed to be mixed with warm water according to the practitioner’s directions.

Pills and capsules

Some formulas are available in pill or capsule form for convenience. Preparation and dosage instructions should be followed exactly as provided.

Chinese herbal medicines can interact with medicines and may cause adverse effects or allergic reactions. Tell the practitioner about medicines, supplements, pregnancy, allergies and relevant health conditions before using herbal products.

Frequently asked questions

Are Chinese herbal medicines safe?

Safety depends on the formula, dose, product quality, medical history and other medicines being used. Herbs should be supplied or recommended by an appropriately qualified practitioner.

How do I take a herbal formula?

Your practitioner will provide preparation and dosage instructions according to the specific formulation supplied.
