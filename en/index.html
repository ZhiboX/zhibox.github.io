Frequently asked questions

What does an acupuncture consultation consider?

A consultation may consider your symptoms, relevant medical history and overall presentation. Whether acupuncture is appropriate depends on an individual assessment.

Does acupuncture hurt?

Acupuncture needles are very fine. Some people feel little discomfort, while others may notice brief sensations during insertion or stimulation.
