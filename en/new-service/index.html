Beauty facial skincare

Beauty facial skincare services are available as individual treatments and may also be combined with cosmetic acupuncture. Please contact Faye Xu to discuss the treatments currently available and whether they are suitable for you.

Cosmetic tattooing

Cosmetic tattooing is performed by a qualified cosmetic tattoo artist. Available services include:

  • Microblading
  • Nano brows
  • Combination ombré shading and nano brows
  • Lash-line eyeliner tattooing
  • Lip tattooing

Pricing and appointment information are available on enquiry.

Cosmetic acupuncture

Cosmetic acupuncture is an appearance-focused technique informed by Chinese medicine meridian theory and modern understanding of facial skin, muscle anatomy and physiology. Specially designed fine needles are applied to selected facial points, skin and muscles.

People may enquire about cosmetic acupuncture for appearance-related concerns such as fine lines, facial contour or skin and muscle tone. Suitability and individual expectations should be discussed with the practitioner before treatment.

Combined treatment package

A combined package of beauty facial skincare and cosmetic acupuncture is available. Treatment is provided by a cosmetic tattoo artist together with a senior AHPRA-registered acupuncturist.

Before booking: Cosmetic and acupuncture procedures have potential risks and are not suitable for everyone. Results vary between individuals. Please disclose relevant health conditions, medicines, allergies, pregnancy and previous cosmetic procedures during your consultation.
